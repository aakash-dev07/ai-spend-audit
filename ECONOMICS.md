# ECONOMICS.md

## What a converted lead is worth to Credex

Credex sells discounted AI infrastructure credits. Assume:
- Average deal: $8,000 ARR (a 20-person startup spending $800/month on AI tools buys discounted credits for $667/month — $8k/year)
- Gross margin on credits: ~20% (sourcing discount minus ops)
- Revenue per deal: $1,600 gross profit/year

A customer who stays 2 years = **$3,200 LTV**.

This is conservative. A 50-person company spending $3k/month in AI tools is a $36k ARR deal.

## Conversion funnel math

```
100 landing page visitors
→ 55 start the audit form (55% — free tool, no friction)
→ 40 complete audit (40% — some abandon mid-form)
→ 12 submit email (30% of completers — post-value capture)
→ 3 book consultation (25% of email submitters, filtered to >$500 savings)
→ 1 buy credits (33% close rate on consultation)
```

**End-to-end: 1% of landing page visitors convert to revenue.**

At $3,200 LTV/customer: need 1 customer/month to break even on tool maintenance costs (~$0 — it's serverless). Need 26 customers/month to hit $1M ARR gross profit.

## What would have to be true for $1M ARR in 18 months

$1M ARR = 125 customers at $8k ARR each (or 42 at $24k ARR, or a mix).

Working backwards from 18 months:
- Month 1–3: 2 customers/month (organic, HN, DM)
- Month 4–9: 8 customers/month (viral loop kicks in, vendor partnerships)
- Month 10–18: 15 customers/month (referral codes, content, inbound from audits shared by existing customers)

**What must be true:**
1. Average deal size grows. The tool attracts a $8k ARR customer on average, but the consultation close rate improves as Credex refines the sales motion → deals average $15k ARR by month 6. Plausible if Credex focuses on 50+ person companies.
2. The viral loop works. Share URLs are used. If 1 in 10 audits shared generates 1 new audit, the organic growth rate compounds meaningfully.
3. Credex closes the vendor partnership with at least one major tool (Cursor or GitHub) to get in-product distribution. This alone could 5× the top-of-funnel.
4. Lead quality improves with benchmark data. By month 6, with 5k audits, the "companies your size spend $X on average" feature is real — this increases urgency to act.

**Key risk:** The tool drives awareness but Credex's sales motion is too slow to convert. Solution: automate the high-savings case → immediately surface a calendar booking link (Calendly embed) rather than waiting for a human follow-up.
