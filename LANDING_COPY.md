## FAQ

**Q: Is this actually free?**
Yes. No credit card, no trial period. SpendLens is a free tool built by Credex. We make money when teams with significant savings switch to Credex credits — but only if it makes sense for you.

**Q: What do you do with my data?**
Your tool and plan data is used to run your audit. If you submit your email, we'll send you the report and — for high-savings cases — our team may reach out about Credex credits. We never sell your data.

**Q: How accurate is the pricing data?**
Every price in the audit engine is verified against the official vendor pricing page, with the date checked. We update it weekly. See PRICING_DATA.md for all sources.

**Q: What if I'm already on the optimal plan?**
We'll tell you. SpendLens doesn't manufacture savings that don't exist. If your stack is well-optimised, you'll get "You're spending well" with a breakdown confirming why.

**Q: What is Credex?**
Credex sells discounted AI infrastructure credits — Cursor, Claude, ChatGPT Enterprise, and others — sourced from companies that overforecast or pivoted. The discount is real. SpendLens surfaces whether those credits make sense for your team.
